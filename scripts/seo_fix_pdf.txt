Humne website ka complete SEO aur technical check kiya aur Google Search mein website ko better 
perform karne ke liye multiple improvements kiye hain. 
 
1. Google ko website samajhne layak banaya 
Pehle kuch product pages Google ko properly nahi dikh rahe the. Google ko page ka actual product content milne ke bajay 
website ka basic/blank structure mil raha tha. 
Ab: Google ko product ka naam, price, description, author, publisher aur other important information properly milti hai. 
Example: 
Agar koi Google par search karta hai “MPPSC Mains Books”, toh Google website ke relevant pages ko properly read aur 
understand kar sakta hai. 
 
 
 
2. Purane product links ko sahi kiya 
Website mein kuch products ke purane URLs change ho gaye the. Is wajah se purane links open karne par product nahi mil 
raha tha. 
Humne 338 old product URLs ko new URLs ke saath connect kiya. 
Example: 
Agar kisi ne purana product link save kiya hua hai: 
old-product-link 
Toh ab automatically: 
Old Link → New Product Page 
par chala jayega. 
Isse customer bhi lost nahi hoga aur Google ko bhi samajh aayega ki product ka new address kya hai. 
 
 
 
3. Broken pages ko properly handle kiya 
Agar koi aisa product URL open karta hai jo website par exist hi nahi karta, toh pehle website kabhi-kabhi normal page ki 
tarah response de rahi thi. 
Ab: 
Product exist karta hai → Product page open hoga 
Product exist nahi karta → Proper 404 page 
Isse Google ko website ke valid aur invalid pages ke beech clear information milti hai.

Sitemap basically Google ke liye website ka map hota hai. 
Isme website ke important pages ki list hoti hai. 
Humne sitemap update kiya aur new SEO/category pages bhi add kiye. 
Currently sitemap mein approximately 628 important URLs hain.

“Shubham Xerox ki website par ye product pages aur ye category pages available hain, inhe crawl/check karo.” 
Live testing mein 50 sample URLs check kiye gaye aur 50/50 properly open hue.

9. Website ka Robots file update kiya 
robots.txt ko simple language mein Google ke liye instructions file samajh sakte ho. 
Humne ensure kiya ki Google: 
✅ Products ko access kar sake 
✅ Categories ko access kar sake 
✅ Important public pages ko crawl kar sake 
Aur private pages jaise: 
❌ Cart 
❌ Checkout 
❌ Admin 
❌ Order Success 
ko unnecessarily crawl na kare. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
10. Website ke duplicate URLs ko control kiya 
Kabhi ek hi page multiple URLs se open ho sakta hai. 
Google ko confusion ho sakta hai ki: 
“Inmein se original page kaunsa hai?” 
Isliye important pages par preferred/original URL set kiya gaya. 
Example: 
Agar same product multiple URLs se access ho raha hai, toh Google ko clearly bataya jata hai: 
“Ye iska main/original URL hai.” 
Isse duplicate-page related problems reduce hoti hain.
