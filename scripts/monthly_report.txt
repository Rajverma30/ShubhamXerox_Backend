Part 1: Technical SEO Foundation (8 – 15 September)
1. Google Ko Website Samajhne Layak Banaya
Pehle kuch product pages Google ko properly visible nahi the. Google ko actual
product content ke bajay website ka basic/blank structure mil raha tha.
Ab Google ko product ka naam, price, description, author, publisher aur baaki zaroori
information properly mil rahi hai.
Example: Agar koi Google par "MPPSC Mains Books" search karta hai, toh Google
website ke relevant pages ko properly read aur samajh sakta hai.
2. Canonical Tag Issue Fix Kiya
Pehle kai product pages ka canonical tag galti se home page par point kar raha tha, jisse
Google unhe "duplicate" ya "less important" samajh raha tha aur unhe sahi ranking nahi
mil rahi thi.
Ab har product page ka canonical tag uske apne correct URL par set hai.
Isse Google ko clearly pata chalta hai ki har product page ek unique, important page
hai, jisse search visibility aur ranking improve hoti hai.
3. Purane Product Links Ko Sahi Kiya (301 Redirects)
Kuch products ke purane URLs change ho gaye the, jiski wajah se purane links par
product nahi milta tha.
Top 15 products ke old URLs ko unke naye clean URLs se 301 permanent redirect ke
through connect kiya gaya. Customer automatically naye product page par pahunch
jaata hai, aur purani backlinks aur search ranking safe rehti hai.
Baaki products ke redirects phase-wise continue ho rahe hain.
4. Broken Pages Ko Properly Handle Kiya
Pehle jo product URL website par exist hi nahi karta tha, wo kabhi-kabhi normal page ki
tarah respond karta tha, jisse Google ko galat signal milta tha.

Part 2: Live Testing, Schema, Robots & Sitemap (15 – 22 September)
1. Website Ka Live Technical Testing
Changes karke chhodne ke bajay live website par sabhi important areas test kiye gaye:
normal pages, product pages, old product URLs, invalid URLs, SEO category pages,
sitemap, robots file, Googlebot access aur HTTPS/domain redirects.
Baaki redirects ki testing next phase mein continue hogi, taaki 338 mein se sabhi verify ho
sakein.
2. Google Ko Products Ke Baare Mein Extra Information (Structured Data)
Website par structured data (schema) add kiya gaya, jisse Google ko product ki
information ek clear, structured format mein milti hai, sirf normal page text se nahi.
Example: Product: XYZ Book | Price: ₹299 | Author: XYZ | Category: MPPSC | ISBN:
XXXXX
Isse Google ko product sahi tarike se samajhne aur search results mein better dikhane
mein madad milti hai.
3. Duplicate URLs Ko Control Kiya
Kabhi-kabhi ek hi page multiple URLs se khulta hai, jisse Google confuse ho sakta hai ki
original kaunsa hai.
Har important page par uska preferred (original) URL set kar diya gaya hai.
Isse duplicate-page ki problems kam hoti hain aur product pages ki ranking behtar hoti
hai.
